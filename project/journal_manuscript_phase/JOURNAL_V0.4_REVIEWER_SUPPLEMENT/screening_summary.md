## Appendix D. Screening scope and selection boundary

The original numeric selection frame contains 614 protocols, partitioned into 48 qualified-contributing, 505 partial, 33 unresolved, 21 excluded and seven other outcomes. The separately grained family aggregate is 102 = 4 numeric-multi + 56 transform + 42 representation-only. The recorded policy ledger has 32 architecture credits and ten complete credits. Its scope is not the numeric-family corpus; a historical Top100 credit attribution remains unresolved. These are qualification counts, not prevalence estimates. The twelve-family/ten-protocol/five-chain numeric breadth requirement was not met by the observed four/four/five. The later stateful study uses a separate prospective selection after earlier results were known [A8].
